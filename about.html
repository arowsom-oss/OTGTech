About OTGTech

A Virtual Enterprise firm that makes the PortaFold.

Who we are

OTGTech is a Virtual Enterprise firm. We make the PortaFold: a case that unfolds into a full desk mat, with wireless charging pads and a power hub built in.

It is made for study, work, travel and home. Open, it needs a 36" surface, like a library table, a dorm desk or a work desk.

Our mission

Our mission is to eliminate clutter and maximize efficiency.

How we built this site

This is a Virtual Enterprise practice store. We used AI tools (Claude, from Anthropic) to help build it, and we want to be clear about where.

  • Code. Much of the site's code was written with AI help, then checked in a browser by the team. The product list and prices and the shop page's color and photo picker were written by a student on the team.
  • Pictures and videos. The PortaFold renders and the fold animations are 3D concept art made with AI-assisted code. They are not photos of a real product, and the final PortaFold may look different.
  • Who decides. The team decides what to build and what the site says, and takes responsibility for it.
  • Read the code. The code for this site is public on GitHub, so anyone can read it: github.com/arowsom-oss/OTGTech.
