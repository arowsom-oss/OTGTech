Why the PortaFold

One case. Every device. Always ready.

Multi-Device Charging

Phone, watch, earbuds and a USB-C device at once, sharing one 65 W power budget.

Smart Organization

The phone stand, watch pad, earbuds pad and every port live together on one panel. No tangled cords.

Two Simple Folds

Three 12" x 18" panels fold into one flat stack, about 12" x 18" x 1". All the electronics sit on one panel, so no wires cross a fold.

Travel-Ready Battery

A 74 Wh battery module is included, under the FAA 100 Wh carry-on limit.

How it works

Open it. Charge. Go.

  1. Open it

    Three 12" x 18" panels unfold into a 36" x 18" desk mat.

  2. Charge

    Your phone goes on the Qi2 stand, your earbuds on their pad, and a USB-C device in the hub. They share 65 W.

  3. Go

    The right panel folds over the middle, then both fold over the left. A flat band holds the stack shut.
