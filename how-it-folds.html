How It Folds

The PortaFold goes from an open desk mat to a flat stack you can strap shut and carry.

What happens, step by step

  1. Unplug. The charging cable comes out of the port on the left edge. The cable is a separate accessory.
  2. Phone off, stand flat. The phone comes off the stand, and the stand folds flat into its pocket.
  3. First fold. The right panel folds over the middle panel.
  4. Second fold. Both fold over the left panel, which holds the charging pads and the power hub.
  5. Strap. A leather band wraps around the stack, with the OTG badge on top.
