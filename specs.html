Power budget

  • Phone pad 25 W
  • Watch pad 5 W
  • Earbuds pad 5 W
  • USB-C port 30 W
  • All at once 65 W

Everything charging at once: phone 25 W + watch 5 W + earbuds 5 W + USB-C 30 W = 65 W.

With the pads idle, the USB-C port can use the full 65 W for a laptop.

The 100 W adapter is recommended because wireless charging loses some energy as heat, so the input needs room above 65 W.

Common questions

Can it charge everything at full speed at once?
Everything shares 65 W. The phone, watch and earbuds pads get power first, and the USB-C port gets the rest, which is 30 W with all pads in use.
Can I fly with it?
Yes. The battery is 74 Wh, under the 100 Wh FAA limit. Keep it in your carry-on, not checked luggage.
What adapter do I need?
A USB-C PD adapter. 100 W is recommended.
